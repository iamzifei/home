A two-ink editorial print plate, very wide — a banner. Flat, front-facing paper
canvas: the image IS the printed sheet, seen straight on, filling the frame edge
to edge.

CANVAS AND INKS
Substrate: pale beige paper, #F5F1E8, clean and contemporary — not aged, not
sepia, not stained. Exactly two printing inks and no third colour:
  Cobalt #2148B8 — dominant, about 70% of the printed area, carrying every
  form and every line.
  Terracotta #C65F38 — accent, under 10%, used in ONE place only.
Any grey is cobalt laid down thinly over the paper, never a neutral grey ink.

COMPOSITION
A PRESS SHEET: one large sheet carrying many small impressions of the same
form, laid out on a strict grid that runs the full width of the banner. The
grid is cropped by the LEFT and RIGHT edges, so the run clearly continues past
the frame in both directions. The top quarter of the banner is bare paper — a
single quiet band with nothing in it. Everything is aligned to that grid; no
element is rotated or scattered.

SUBJECT
Roughly twenty-four small rectangles in four rows, each the proportion of an
application window: a thin bar across the top with three tiny circles at its
left, and a body below it. Most are drawn as OUTLINE only. About six of them
have bodies filled with a coarse halftone dot screen, at different densities,
scattered through the grid rather than grouped. Exactly ONE rectangle, left of
centre in the second row, is filled solid terracotta — it is the only warm mark
on the sheet. Two registration crosses sit in the outer margins.

TEXT
No text of any kind. No words, no labels, no numbers, no logos, no signature,
no watermark. The page this plate belongs to sets its own type; type printed
into the picture would be set twice.

MATERIAL AND PROHIBITIONS
Screenprint / risograph reproduction: visible halftone dots at close range,
uneven ink density across the sheet, clipped highlights where the paper shows
through, about 1mm of registration drift between the two plates. Absolutely no
mockup: no desk under the sheet, no photograph of paper, no drop shadow, no
curled corner, no frame, no border, no background surface of any kind. No
people, no hands, no devices, no perspective, no 3D, no gradient mesh, no glow.
