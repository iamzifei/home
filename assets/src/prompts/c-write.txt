A two-ink editorial print plate. Flat, front-facing paper canvas — the image IS
the printed sheet, seen straight on, filling the frame edge to edge.

CANVAS AND INKS
Substrate: pale beige paper, #F5F1E8, clean and contemporary — not aged, not
sepia, not stained. Exactly two printing inks and no third colour:
  Cobalt #2148B8 — dominant, roughly 80% of the printed area, carrying the
  image and every structural line.
  Terracotta #C65F38 — accent, under 15%, with ONE job, in ONE place.
Any grey is cobalt laid thinly over the paper, never a neutral grey ink.

TEXT
No text of any kind. No words, no labels, no numbers, no logos, no signature,
no watermark. Where writing would appear, print SOLID OR HALFTONE BARS standing
in for lines of type — greeking, never legible characters.

MATERIAL AND PROHIBITIONS
Screenprint / risograph reproduction: visible halftone dots at close range,
uneven ink density, clipped highlights where the paper shows through, about 1mm
of registration drift between the two plates. Absolutely no mockup: no desk
under the sheet, no photograph of paper, no drop shadow, no curled corner, no
frame, no border, no background surface. No people, no faces, no hands. No
gradient mesh, no glow, no 3D render, no photographic depth.

COMPOSITION
Portrait sheet. One dominant object occupying about 55% of the area, anchored to
the LEFT edge and cropping off the BOTTOM edge. About 45% bare paper, gathered
as one quiet region across the top.

SUBJECT
A single printed page seen straight on, slightly rotated a few degrees off
square, carrying a column of type rendered as SOLID COBALT BARS of uneven length
— never letters. Two of the bars are halftone rather than solid. A wide margin
runs down the left side of the page and is empty. One short horizontal rule,
printed in terracotta, sits in that empty margin beside the third bar from the
top and is the ONLY terracotta mark on the sheet. Below the page, a second sheet
peeks out by a few millimetres.
