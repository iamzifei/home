A two-ink editorial print plate, very wide — a banner. Flat, front-facing paper
canvas: the image IS the printed sheet, seen straight on, filling the frame edge
to edge.

CANVAS AND INKS
Substrate: pale beige paper, #F5F1E8, clean and contemporary — not aged, not
sepia, not stained. Exactly two printing inks and no third colour:
  Cobalt #2148B8 — dominant, about 75% of the printed area, carrying every
  form and every line.
  Terracotta #C65F38 — accent, under 8%, used in ONE place only.
Any grey is cobalt laid down thinly over the paper, never a neutral grey ink.

COMPOSITION
A single horizontal rule runs the full width of the banner, sitting about two
thirds of the way down: a table edge. Standing upright ON that rule, in one
even row, are ten identical sheets of paper seen straight on, portrait
proportion, evenly spaced. The row is cropped by the LEFT and RIGHT edges so it
clearly continues past the frame in both directions. The band of paper above
the sheets is bare — a quiet strip with nothing in it at all. Everything sits
on the same baseline; no sheet is rotated, tilted, overlapped or scattered.

SUBJECT
Each of the ten sheets is drawn as OUTLINE only, a thin cobalt rectangle, and
carries four or five short ruled lines across its upper half and one small
empty box near its foot — a worksheet, a form waiting to be filled in. The
ruled lines vary slightly in length from sheet to sheet, the way a hand-ruled
run does. Exactly ONE sheet, the fourth from the left, is filled solid
terracotta with its ruled lines knocked out in the bare paper — it is the only
warm mark on the plate. Two small registration crosses sit in the outer
margins, one at each end of the table rule.

TEXT
No text of any kind. No words, no letters, no labels, no numbers, no logos, no
signature, no watermark. The ruled lines are LINES, not writing. The page this
plate belongs to sets its own type; type printed into the picture would be set
twice and could not be translated.

MATERIAL AND PROHIBITIONS
Screenprint / risograph reproduction: visible halftone dots at close range,
uneven ink density across the sheet, clipped highlights where the paper shows
through, about 1mm of registration drift between the two plates. Absolutely no
mockup: no desk under the sheet, no photograph of paper, no drop shadow, no
curled corner, no frame, no border, no background surface of any kind. No
people, no hands, no chairs, no devices, no perspective, no 3D, no gradient
mesh, no glow.
